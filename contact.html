Sandy Kahrod is a basketmaker working the edges of Birmingham.

She weaves with bramble, willow and whatever else the city’s unmanaged ground gives up — railway embankments, canal towpaths, verges, the scrub behind retail parks. Brambles are the plant of neglected land, which makes them the honest material for a maker with no willow field and no rural inheritance to draw on.

She came to basketry as an adult, outside the lineage British basketry usually claims for itself.
